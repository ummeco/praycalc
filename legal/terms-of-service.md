## 7. Location Data

PrayCalc requires location information to calculate accurate prayer times. By using location features, you agree to the following:

- **GPS location:** PrayCalc may request device GPS access on mobile apps. This is used solely to compute prayer times and Qibla direction. GPS data is not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save a location.
- **IP-based geolocation:** If GPS is unavailable or not granted, PrayCalc may use your IP address to approximate your location at city-level precision.
- **Manual location:** You may enter a city or coordinates manually; no device location permission is used in this case.
- **Retention:** Saved locations (including your "home" location) are stored in your Account if you are signed in. You may delete saved locations at any time from your account settings.

For full details on how location data is handled, see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy), specifically the "Location Data" section.
