## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 1, 2026
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26
**Version:** 1.0.0

This policy applies to PrayCalc at praycalc.com and the PrayCalc iOS, Android, and desktop
apps operated by Ummeco LLC. For all base terms, see the shared base policy. The sections
below describe what is specific to PrayCalc.

Questions? Email privacy@praycalc.com.

---

### 1. Who We Are

Ummeco LLC operates PrayCalc at praycalc.com and the PrayCalc mobile and desktop apps.

**Privacy contact:** privacy@praycalc.com
**GDPR data subject requests:** dpa@ummat.dev

---

### 2. Location Data (PrayCalc-Specific)

PrayCalc needs location data to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Here is
how that works:

**Coarse location (default, no prompt):** By default, we derive your location from your IP
address to a ZIP code or city level. This is accurate enough for prayer time calculation and
requires no device permission. We do not store your IP address beyond standard server log
retention (30 days, then anonymized).

**Precise GPS (opt-in, with explicit consent):** On mobile apps, you can grant location
permission for higher accuracy (within meters). We only request this permission when you
actively tap "Use my precise location." We store the coordinates you permit us to store for
your convenience; you can delete them at any time in Settings. We do not share your GPS
coordinates with any third party.

**Manual city entry:** You can enter any city name manually. No device permission needed.
We store your entered city in your account preferences.

**What we never do with location data:**

- We do not combine location data with other data to build profiles
- We do not share location data with advertisers
- We do not use location data for any purpose other than prayer time and Qibla calculation

---

### 3. Content You Submit (PrayCalc-Specific)

PrayCalc collects:

- Prayer time calculation settings (calculation method, madhab, Asr factor, adjustments)
- Optional prayer log entries you record manually (prayer completed yes/no, optional note)
- City or location preference
- App notification preferences

Prayer log entries are private to your account. We do not read, aggregate across users, or
share them.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

PrayCalc does not target children. The service is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do
not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
data without parental consent, contact privacy@praycalc.com and we will delete it promptly.

---

### 5. Data Retention (PrayCalc Additions)

| Data class | Retention | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Prayer log entries | Until account deleted or user deletes them | User-owned data |
| Location preference (city) | Until user changes or deletes it | App functionality |
| Precise GPS coordinates (if consented) | Until user revokes consent or deletes account | Saved preference |
| Notification tokens (iOS/Android) | Until device deregistered or account deleted | Push delivery |
